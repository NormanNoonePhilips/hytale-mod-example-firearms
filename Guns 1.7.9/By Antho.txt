This mod unlocks and makes usable the firearms hidden in Hytale’s game files, fully integrating them into the gameplay. It introduces a new craftable Ammo item, required to use all firearms. The Hand Gun and Assault Rifle are both fully craftable and use this Ammo system. Weapons and ammunition: can be crafted using the standard Workbench, in exchange for Powder.
